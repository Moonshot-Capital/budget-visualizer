How it works

Three steps to see exactly where your money goes. No account, no upload – everything stays in your browser.

1

Set up your profile

Enter your monthly net income (or your annual salary – the other value is calculated for you), pick a currency and optionally your job title. Use the Monthly / Yearly toggle in the header to switch the whole view.

2

Add your expenses

Type a description and an amount, or tap a quick-add chip. Tap any expense to edit it, use the arrows or drag to reorder, and “Sort by amount” for an instant ranking. Deleted something by accident? Hit Undo in the toast.

3

Read, share, export

The bar and the KPI cards (expenses, surplus, savings rate, largest item) update live. Tap a segment for details. Export a PDF report, three Instagram story slides, or copy a link that encodes your whole budget – open it on any device to continue there.
